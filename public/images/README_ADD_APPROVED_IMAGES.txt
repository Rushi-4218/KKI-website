KKI IMAGE POLICY FOR THIS BUILD
===============================

No brochure crop, AI-style equipment mockup, or unapproved stock image is used by the redesigned homepage.

When approved Kitchen King product / facility / marine images are supplied, they can be integrated into:
- Homepage hero
- Product category cards
- About / manufacturing section
- Marine & galley section
- Catalogue / campaign sections

Existing database/admin-uploaded product images are NOT removed and continue to be supported by ProductCard.
